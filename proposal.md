## Description
I am planning to create a landscape using small points or squares. These points will appear similarly to the animation from the previous lab, 
where each one drops down into its assigned position along with its corresponding color. As more points appear, they will gradually come together 
to form a complete landscape image. Once the image is fully assembled, it will remain on screen for a few seconds before all the points are pulled 
back down and disappear, ending the animation.
